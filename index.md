## About Me
I am a Research Assistant on the Financial Economics team at the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago.

Before joining the Chicago Fed, I attended Dartmouth College where I majored in economics and graduated with high honors.  My main roles on campus were as a research assistant (RA) and a teaching assistant (TA).  During my freshman and sophomore years, I worked with Assistant Professor Xin Qi as a pharmaceutical engineering RA developing antibodies to target Interleukin-33.  Most recently, I worked with The Dartmouth Professor in Economics Erzo Luttmer as a public economics RA looking at valuing human lives based on respondents’ choice between vignettes of life stories where life-time income and age of death are randomized.  During my junior winter, I completed an independent health econonmics research project looking at the long-term effects of the ACA Medicaid expansions.  For my senior thesis, I studied the effects of gun policy on gun violence and housing prices.
